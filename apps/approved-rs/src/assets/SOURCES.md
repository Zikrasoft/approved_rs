## flags/

Rasterised from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) (MIT) 4×3
SVGs at 72×48 WebP. The SVGs themselves were too heavy to ship: Serbia is
181 KB and Spain 81 KB, almost all of it a coat of arms that is invisible at
the 24×16 the plate renders. `en.webp` is the flag of the United Kingdom.
The package is not a dependency — regenerate with sharp if a country is added.
