      A few days later, the customer contacted us again. The weather had turned colder, and he noticed an unpleasant problem: the heater was blowing warm air on one side of the cabin and cold air on the other.

      This is a familiar issue for owners of **Volkswagen, Audi, Skoda and SEAT** vehicles. One possible cause is a clogged heater core, including a blockage caused by silica gel particles from the coolant expansion tank.

      Some VAG vehicles have a special silica gel pack inside the coolant expansion tank. If the pack is damaged, particles can enter the cooling system and gradually clog the narrow passages in the heater core. This disrupts coolant flow and causes the heater to warm the cabin unevenly.

      Uneven heating can also be caused by other faults, such as problems with the climate control flaps. That's why it's important to identify the cause correctly before starting repairs.
