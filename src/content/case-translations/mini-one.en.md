**Car sourcing in Serbia — another successful search for our client.**

Stanislav came to us with an interesting challenge. Despite being **195 cm** tall, after trying out a range of cars it turned out a **Mini** actually suited him best for seating position and comfort. The main requirement was low mileage and good technical condition.

The search turned up a **Mini One** with only **60,000 km**. Just **two repainted panels**, a great service history confirmed via **BMW AIR**, and a solid **CarVertical** report with no red flags.

The one downside was a genuinely dirty interior — the previous owner hadn't kept up with cleaning. After a professional detailing job, which Stanislav handled himself, the Mini was transformed: the interior looks brand new, smells great, and the car now makes its owner happy every day.
